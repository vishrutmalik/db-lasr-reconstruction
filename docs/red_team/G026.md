  (A-G023-08 realization; RT-G023-2 ratchet-flip genuineness). The central
  instrument is an INDEPENDENT overlap recompute — for every (retained

  probe guards a detector (the delayed-mode perturbation teeth initially

  promoted to `tests/leakage/test_red_team_g026_walkforward_attacks.py`
  (8 keepers + 3 strict-xfail ratchets).
- **Relation to the verification report** (`docs/verification/G026.md`,
  PASS, 5 non-blocking; written in parallel and independently): none of
  their findings coincide with RT-G026-1/2/3 (all three are new); their
  non-blocking 1 (zero-test-row folds run silently) is adjacent to my N8
  (vanished names invisible in the PredictionSet) — the two together say

## Verdict

**0 BLOCKING findings.** 3 ratcheted non-blocking defects (RT-G026-1,
RT-G026-2, RT-G026-3), 11 recorded notes/observations. The purge/embargo
core survived every attack available to the four EVIDENCED family
configurations: the strict-at-A boundary shares a price point, never a
return segment, in all four execution modes (independently recomputed);
post-A price perturbation cannot move the retained training set; on
backcast folds the e=1 embargo is EXACT for uniform symmetric families
(zero under- and zero over-exclusions — the +k execution shift cancels
because train `target_start` and test `target_end` are computed by the
same `shift_trading_days` call); the clock can never stamp a future fit
(bisect-≤ plus `TimingRecord` re-validation); the CI-006 pair guards abort
rather than leak; determinism holds under double-run and record-order
permutation. All three ratcheted defects live on configuration edges no
evidenced config reaches (`close_to_open` basis; mixed-family record
pools; sub-horizon embargo) — but each produces GENUINE shared return
segments between retained training rows and test outcomes when reached,
and each contradicts the folds-module docstring claim that "at e = 1
purge+embargo together exclude exactly every training window sharing a
return segment with any test outcome". They must be fixed (or refused at
configuration time) before any of those edges is exercised by G025/G032+.

## Findings

### RT-G026-1 — `close_to_open` H=1 backcast folds retain training rows sharing real return segments with test outcomes (NON-BLOCKING, ratcheted; inherits RT-G023-1)

`select_training_records` protects the test period with (i) a purge keyed
on the test DECISION instants `(A_close, B_close)` and (ii) an embargo
gated on `record.overlap.horizon_steps > 1`. A `close_to_open` window ends
at the open FOLLOWING the next grid point (RT-G023-1's one-overnight
extension), so on a backcast fold (train after test — the shape where the
embargo is the only defense) the first post-test train row starts at a
close that lies strictly INSIDE the last test rows' windows; with H=1 the
embargo is skipped entirely and the purge cannot see past `B_close`.
Probe 2b: 25 retained pairs, e.g. retained train `s01@2020-03-06`
`(Mar-06 close, Mar-16 open]` vs test `s01@2020-02-28`
`(Feb-28 close, Mar-09 open]` — the Mar-06→Mar-09 weekend gap is realized
in BOTH. For H>1 `close_to_open` families the per-row calendar `H_row`
happens to cover the overnight, so only H=1 leaks. Falsifies the module
docstring's exactness claim (folds.py lines 29–31). Why non-blocking:
`close_to_open` is no `DEFAULT_BASIS`, no evidenced config uses it, and
G023's RT-1 ratchet already blocks its metadata; it becomes blocking the
moment that basis is run through validation. Fix direction: intersect
against actual test-row window extents (or refuse the basis at fold
selection, mirroring the RT-G023-1 fix).
Ratchet: `test_rt1_close_to_open_h1_backcast_must_not_retain_overlapping_rows`
(strict xfail; accepts refusal or exclusion as the fix).

### RT-G026-2 — mixed-horizon record pools bypass the embargo through the `horizon_steps == 1` gate (NON-BLOCKING, ratcheted)

Nothing refuses records of DIFFERENT horizons in one selection call (the
CI-015d check compares `overlap_mode` only, and both 1M and 3M evidenced
families are `pooled_as_paper`). The embargo zone is per-row —
`(B_close, B_close + e·H_row]` with `H_row` the TRAIN row's own duration —
but the quantity that needs covering is the TEST rows' outcome extension.
Probe 2c: 1M+3M records in one backcast fold are accepted silently and
produce 75 retained-1M/test-3M shared-segment pairs; a retained 1M train
row `(Jun-30, Jul-31]` sits fully inside the 3M test outcome
`(Apr-30, Jul-31]` — its embargo check never even runs
(`horizon_steps == 1`). For uniform families the per-row zone is provably
exact (attack 2e), so the defect is confined to mixing. Why non-blocking:
no sanctioned path pools families in one selection (each version has one
target family; G025 selectors are per-family) — this is a missing
defense-in-depth refusal on a public function whose docstring does not
forbid the input. Fix direction: refuse horizon-heterogeneous record sets
(one line, mirroring the overlap-mode check) or embargo against
`max(H_row over the pool ∪ {test rows})`.
Ratchet: `test_rt2_mixed_horizon_fold_must_refuse_or_exclude_short_row_overlaps`
(strict xfail; accepts refusal or exclusion).

### RT-G026-3 — sub-horizon embargo (0 < e < 1) accepted silently on overlapping families and under-excludes (NON-BLOCKING, ratcheted)

CI-015(b) specifies "an embargo of AT LEAST ONE FULL HORIZON after the
test period is configurable and defaults ON for overlapping families".
`FoldSpec`/`generate_folds`/`TargetFamilySpec` all accept any
`embargo_horizons >= 0`; probe 2d (e=0.5, uniform 3M, backcast) runs with
no refusal and no warning and retains 25 rows sharing whole months of
return segment with test outcomes (e.g. retained `(Jul-31, Oct-30]` vs
test `(May-29, Aug-31]`). e=0 (embargo explicitly OFF) is arguably a
legal config switch; 0 < e < 1 purports the embargo is ON while
delivering less than the criterion's floor — the house style for such
configs is refusal, never a warning (LT-012 precedent). Why non-blocking:
the default is 1.0 everywhere, no evidenced config sets e < 1, and the
zone binds only on backcast folds. Fix direction: refuse
`0 < embargo_horizons < 1` when `horizon_steps > 1` (config-time, like
`ensure_purge_admissible`).
Ratchet: `test_rt3_sub_horizon_embargo_must_be_refused_or_still_cover`
(strict xfail).

## Notes and observations (no defect / deferred obligations)

- **N1 (refit anchor is plan-bounds-relative, A-G026-04):** the refit grid
  anchors on the first rebalance day of `plan.bounds()`; adding or
  removing an EARLIER fold phase-shifts every later fold's fit stamps
  (probe 3a: decision 2020-06-30 fit at Jun-30 vs Apr-30 under a one-point
  window shift). Never a future fit (keeper-pinned); deterministic within
  one plan; but two plans sharing a fold do not share its fits —
  cross-experiment comparability hazard worth a register line.
- **N2 (asymmetric off-grid strictness):** `Clock.decision_time` refuses
  off-grid days; `Clock.model_fit_time` silently bisects them
  (conservative ≤). The runner passes `fold.test.start`, which nothing
  requires to be a grid day.
- **N3 (same_close still unflagged, A-G026-05):** the fully collapsed
  chain (`signal == decision == execution`) flows through fold selection,
  fitting, and prediction with truthful stamps and zero runtime warnings —
  RT-G023-N4's gap now spans two layers. The eventual banner should live
  wherever results are aggregated (G028) at the latest.
- **N4 (duplicates double-count silently):** selection neither dedups nor
  refuses duplicate `(security_id, as_of)` rows; PK uniqueness is an
  upstream dataset guarantee that direct callers can bypass.
- **N5 (`row.as_of` conflated with the decision instant):** the runner
  groups test records and queries models at `signal_time = row.as_of`
  while stamping `signal_time = decision_time`; for schema-legal
  early-knowledge rows (`as_of < decision_time`) the CI-006 predict guard
  ABORTS the run (conservative, probe 4d). Safe today because G023 pins
  `as_of == decision_time`; the schema does not.
- **N6 (cross-config records unchecked):** records carrying a foreign
  `config_hash` run under any plan; the PredictionSet is stamped with the
  plan's hash. Provenance-only; a one-line equality check would close it.
- **N7 (no defensive copy of `records`):** the runner re-scans the
  caller's sequence per fold; a record appended mid-run (buggy
  orchestration) is scored in later folds (probe 5b: phantom predicted in
  fold g1). Frozen types block mutation THROUGH the runner's own
  hand-outs; the list itself is shared state.
- **N8 (vanished names are invisible in the PredictionSet — O-4 /
  A-G023-08):** a halt-then-delist name produces no record at the skip
  decision, hence no prediction AND no `UnscoredEvent`; only the G023 skip
  ledger (`missing_end_price`) and listing intervals carry the fact. G026
  loses nothing it was given (the scored-record case carries
  `delisted_in_window` + intact `holding_end` — verifier-confirmed and
  re-checked), but G027 MUST consume those sources for terminal-return
  realization; keeper pins the interface fact. Pairs with the verifier's
  non-blocking 1 (zero-test-row folds are equally silent).
- **N9 (RNG spawn by fold position):** permuting fold order re-deals the
  child streams (documented rule). Fold-id-keyed spawning would make
  results invariant to plan ordering; plan order is frozen config, so this
  is a robustness nicety.
- **N10 (tail drop + OOS containment-not-coverage):** trailing grid points
  that fit no full test window vanish from the fold set with only an INFO
  count; a plan's declared `oos_window` may extend past every fold's test
  range and still validate — the reported OOS claim can overstate actual
  test coverage. G028's reporting should reconcile claimed vs covered.
- **N11 (seasonal "12 years" = 12 distinct present years):** a year with
  no grid day in the month stretches the window to 13 calendar years for
  12 samples — deterministic, but a P1-20 reading choice worth a register
  line before G025 consumes it.
- **N12 (overlapping test windows across folds):** `step_steps <
  test_steps` legally predicts the same (security, as_of) outcome in up to
  `test_steps` folds; predictions are fold-keyed, so downstream
  aggregation (G028) must dedup or weight to avoid double-counting.
