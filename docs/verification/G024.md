## 8. A-G024-03 ruling (no coverage renormalization in Z)

The hazard is REAL and larger than "flagged for audit" might suggest: Z sums
√(W⁺W⁻) over covered mass only, so a factor with coverage c has Z scaled by
≈c. Verifier probes: a pure-noise factor at 40% coverage scores Z≈0.206 —
lower than a genuinely discriminative full-coverage factor — and a
40%-coverage copy of the SAME signal beats its full-coverage twin in every
round. On any real multi-factor panel with uneven coverage, min-Z selection
will be dominated by low-coverage factors regardless of signal.

Ruling: **not a pre-merge blocker for G024**, for three reasons: (1) the
formula is faithful to P1's silence — the evidence (`formulas.md` §3,
spec §6) defines Z on the covered masses and P1 never mentions coverage
adjustment; inventing a renormalization here would violate the project's own
CI-044/no-silent-choices discipline; (2) every G024 consumer surface in this
PR (synthetic tests, LT-005 single-factor) is full-coverage or
single-candidate, so no current result is corrupted; (3) the hazard is
honestly flagged in the assumption register, kernel comments, PR body, and
coordination notes (G037 audit item). HOWEVER, deferral to a G037 *audit* is
too weak given the probe magnitudes — see R-1: this must be a **named
pre-condition for G025/G029** (uneven-coverage panels are exactly what the
ensemble/vertical-slice goals introduce), resolved either by a documented
coverage floor on candidate factors or an explicit
`z_coverage_renormalization` config knob with an A/B test, before any
multi-factor uneven-coverage training run is interpreted.
