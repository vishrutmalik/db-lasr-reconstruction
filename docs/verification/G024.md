## 8. A-G024-03 ruling (no coverage renormalization in Z)

The hazard is REAL and larger than "flagged for audit" might suggest: Z sums
√(W⁺W⁻) over covered mass only, so a factor with coverage c has Z scaled by
≈c. Verifier probes: a pure-noise factor at 40% coverage scores Z≈0.206 —
lower than a genuinely discriminative full-coverage factor — and a
40%-coverage copy of the SAME signal beats its full-coverage twin in every
round. On any real multi-factor panel with uneven coverage, min-Z selection
will be dominated by low-coverage factors regardless of signal.

Ruling: **not a pre-merge blocker for G024**, for three reasons: (1) the
formula is faithful to P1's silence — the evidence (`formulas.md` §3,
spec §6) defines Z on the covered masses and P1 never mentions coverage
adjustment; inventing a renormalization here would violate the project's own
CI-044/no-silent-choices discipline; (2) every G024 consumer surface in this
PR (synthetic tests, LT-005 single-factor) is full-coverage or
single-candidate, so no current result is corrupted; (3) the hazard is
honestly flagged in the assumption register, kernel comments, PR body, and
coordination notes (G037 audit item). HOWEVER, deferral to a G037 *audit* is
too weak given the probe magnitudes — see R-1: this must be a **named
pre-condition for G025/G029** (uneven-coverage panels are exactly what the
ensemble/vertical-slice goals introduce), resolved either by a documented
coverage floor on candidate factors or an explicit
`z_coverage_renormalization` config knob with an A/B test, before any
multi-factor uneven-coverage training run is interpreted.

## 11. Findings

### Blocking

None.

### Non-blocking

- **NB-1** — `missing_policy="propagate_nan"` with NaN ranks in TRAINING data
  makes the selected factor's `h` NaN and the loop dies in `renormalize` with
  `weight mass must be finite and positive to renormalize, got nan` — a loud
  error, but one that names neither the factor nor the policy. A-G024-04
  documents the h_zero training path only; the propagate_nan training
  consequence (config leaf is `missing_at_predict`, yet the policy also
  binds at the training weight update) deserves one sentence in the kernel
  docstring and/or a friendlier error. Cosmetic robustness; the default path
  is unaffected.
- **NB-2** — LT-005's documented criterion "factor selected in ≥ 50% of all
  boosting rounds" is structurally guaranteed on the world-native
  single-factor pool (3900/3900) and, per the recorded observation +
  verifier re-measurement, would NOT hold with distractor candidates
  (26.7% on the verifier's seed; the model IC also dipped to 0.0673, below
  the documented band). The doc's "selected in most rounds" phrasing needs a
  scenario-owner ruling (G037 queue, as the implementer proposed). The flip
  itself is honest against the catalog scenario as constructed; the
  criterion is weaker than the doc implies.
- **NB-3** — `deserialize_fitted_model` accepts `train_row_count` via
  `isinstance(row_count, int)`, which admits `bool` (Python bool ⊂ int);
  `True` would deserialize as row count 1. Unreachable from
  `serialize_fitted_model` output; pedantic input-validation nit.
- **NB-4** — evidence rows P1-10..18 Code/Test cells remain `TBD` in
  read-only `docs/evidence/p1_nlasr_2012/evidence_rows.md`; the implementer
  correctly did not edit a read-only path and listed proposed updates for
  the doc owner. Needs a follow-up doc-owner commit so the evidence table
  reaches the skill's exit criterion ("evidence rows carry code/test refs").

### Recommendations

- **R-1** (strengthens A-G024-03 handling): make the coverage-bias
  resolution a named pre-condition of G025/G029 dispatch — not just a G037
  audit line — via coverage floor or `z_coverage_renormalization` config
  with an A/B test. The verifier's probe (§6 i/i2) shows selection is
  effectively coverage-ordered, not signal-ordered, under uneven coverage.
- **R-2**: when G025 consumes the kernel, add a training-path test for
  `propagate_nan` + missing data pinning the intended behavior (hard error
  with a policy-naming message, or a documented ban on propagate_nan in
  training).
